# Junior Business Analyst

## Professional profile
Junior Business Analyst focused on identifying business needs, communicating with stakeholders, analyzing requirements, and translating them into clear, structured documentation. Interested in optimizing business processes, aligning stakeholder expectations, and supporting the development of effective software solutions.

## Key Skills
* **Requirements:** Functional and non-functional requirements, requirements elicitation, user stories (INVEST), use cases, and acceptance criteria.
* **Technical:** SDLC fundamentals and basic understanding of version control (Git/GitHub).
* **Tools:** Jira, Confluence (Currently learning) Trello, Figma, and GitHub.
* **Agile:** Scrum, Kanban, Sprint planning, Backlog management, and WIP limits.
* **Documentation:** Business Requirements Document (BRD), Software Requirements Specification (SRS), business rules, and change management.
